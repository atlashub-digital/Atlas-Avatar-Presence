# AAP-VISION-001 · Human-facing presence for digital Experts

**Status:** PROPOSED product direction; prototype baseline **IMPLEMENTED** as synthetic demo.

## Why
AI-WaaS agents should appear as credible, identifiable companions: animated face/body, voice and contextual assistance, not just text bubbles. Users must know *which agent* is active and when it listens, speaks or requests an action.

## Opportunities
- **Atlas Experts**: a business Expert accompanies a client or employee at their own computer.
- **Helena**: first internal Windows test using existing Hermes identity; no duplicate profile/LLM.
- **LIA**: brand-specific wellbeing companion, strict separation from AtlasHub internal knowledge; no independent medical diagnosis.
- **Professor**: teaching companion on controlled browser/TV app; safeguarding and guardian controls for children.
- **Real estate/legal/healthcare**: role- and jurisdiction-specific intelligence remains in AI Workforce + Knowledge Hub; presentation uses distinct branded manifests.

## Precise MVP
Windows 10/11 x64 sandbox; user manually activates avatar and conversation, character visible above desktop; animation / listen / think / speak; verified link to existing Hermes session or safe text mock fallback; no screen capture; visible microphone state; graceful disconnect; CPU/RAM and response latency measured.

## Non-goals until POC
Photorealistic face clone; full-body commercial digital human; arbitrary desktop control; generic iOS overlay; access to patients' data; unsupervised marketing publishing; commercial TV tutor; own LLM orchestration or second memory.
